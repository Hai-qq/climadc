# WeatherDC source evidence card

## Frozen input evidence

The immutable local archive inspected for this migration is
`WeatherDC-MVP.zip` with SHA-256:

```text
0264d7b7db762fbc7aebbf63f6afa00cc7a72f6c4449a5fea2c5f26ff98ef5e8
```

No file from its `data/processed`, generated artifact, model, or joblib directories is included in
ClimaDC. The checked test data are independently generated by
`tests/fixtures/weatherdc_small/generate_fixture.py` and dedicated to the public domain under
CC0-1.0.

The repository benchmark configuration and synthetic workload belong only to the offline small
fixture. The `--full` path converts climate observations and DC telemetry, writes a cryptographic
conversion manifest, and produces no workload, model predictions, scheduler result, or benchmark.

## Site and schema context

- Data-center coordinates recorded by WeatherDC: 13.847050, 100.571942.
- HII station: BST1 at 13.6777, 100.7945.
- Local timezone: `Asia/Bangkok`; canonical ClimaDC timestamps are UTC.
- Meter inputs: CRAC3, CRAC4, ULC5, and ULC6 active three-phase power.
- Weather inputs: temperature, humidity, pressure, rain, and solar radiation.
- The archive describes DC meter coverage from 2018-05-16 through 2018-07-10 and HII station
  history from 2009 through 2018.

## Rights and limitations

The source manifest identifies where the files were published; it does not grant ClimaDC the right
to relicense or redistribute them. Users must consult the current energydata and HII terms at the
upstream locations. The original data card identifies the roughly 30.5 km station/DC separation as
a material spatial mismatch. No job, inlet-temperature, control-action, or online-savings evidence
is present, so this study must not be interpreted as a production-control validation.
